A note from the owner

"Beauty already exists. It's been here for millions of years. How we harness that — through nature, and away from heaps of over-produced 'trending' products — will absolutely start to heal us."

Flynn & Phoenix is a small shop made and run by a stay-at-home mom. I wanted a cleaner life for myself and my children — not just cleaner in the foods that enrich us or the medicines that heal us, but clean in our souls, bodies, and minds too.

Everything here is pulled from nature or recycled from someone else's life. A little of everything, with one rule: second hand, vintage, or handmade. My art reflects how I feel about life and death, and how beauty isn't something you have to chase by following a trend.

Healing starts small. It begins with supporting a small business, and it ends with all of us finding art in nature, in life and death, in eating well, in recycling, in whole foods and medicinals. You don't have to buy anything. Just look, and get inspired.

With love, Johnna Owner, artist & mother — Flynn & Phoenix
